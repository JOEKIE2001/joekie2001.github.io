Research interests

Architected materials & mechanical metamaterials · interpenetrating phase composites (IPCs) · triply periodic minimal surface (TPMS) topologies · high strain-rate impact mechanics · dynamic mechanical analysis (modal / harmonic / transmissibility) · additive manufacturing & hybrid fabrication · finite element analysis · machine learning for materials design

Research projects

Vibration suppression with mechanical metastructures

Ph.D. research, NYU / NYUAD · October 2025 – present

  • Quantitative review of 70+ architected metastructure designs in five mechanism-based categories, with an Ashby-type map linking load-bearing efficiency and vibration attenuation.
  • Designed, 3D-printed and tested chiral-enhanced TPMS metastructures; shaker-based transmissibility tests correlated with ANSYS modal and harmonic simulations.

Static and dynamic performance of architected materials and structures

Ph.D. research, NYU / NYUAD · April 2025 – present

  • Experimental modal analysis (mode shapes, natural frequencies, damping ratios) of single- and dual-phase IPC beams with four TPMS architectures and one stochastic architecture.
  • First experimental evidence of vibration-response mechanisms in dual-polymer-phase IPC beams, with system damping ratios up to 16–18% at stiffness comparable to single-phase beams.

Hybrid manufacturing and high-rate impact behavior of architected IPCs

Ph.D. research, NYU / NYUAD · July 2024 – March 2025 · in collaboration with the EC Joint Research Centre (ELSA-HopLab), Ispra, Italy

  • Aluminum–polymer IPCs (BCC, Gyroid, IWP, Spinodal) made by selective laser sintering plus vacuum-assisted polymer infiltration.
  • Quasi-static to dynamic compression (0.001–400 s−1) on one of the world's largest Split Hopkinson Pressure Bar systems; compressive strengths of 250–290 MPa at 400 s−1.
  • Nonlinear Ashby-type surrogate models (R² > 0.95) predicting stiffness and specific strength from strain rate, topology and phase composition.

Multi-material additive manufacturing: separation of metal powders

Master's research, NUS / NUSRI · September 2022 – June 2024

  • Electromagnet-assisted system for recovering mixed metal powders (316L–Cu, Co–Cu, Ni–Cu) from multi-material laser powder bed fusion.

Cu–CuxO porous anode for lithium-ion batteries

Undergraduate research, Sichuan University · July 2020 – May 2021

  • 3D porous copper anode made by dealloying a Cu35Mn alloy; characterized with TEM, XRD and SEM.
